Effective Date: March 2026

Program Description

Ironstone Contracting LLC operates a two-way SMS translation service for business communication with Spanish-speaking contractors and business contacts. Messages are translated between English and Spanish to facilitate business operations.

How to Opt In

You may opt in to receive SMS messages from Ironstone Contracting LLC by texting START to our business SMS number. By opting in you agree to receive business-related translated SMS messages from Ironstone Contracting LLC.

How to Opt Out

You may opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message. You will receive one confirmation message and no further messages will be sent.

Help

For help, reply HELP to any message or contact Ironstone Contracting LLC directly through prior business communication channels.

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies based on business communication needs.

Message & Data Rates

Message and data rates may apply depending on your mobile carrier plan.

Supported Carriers

Service is available on all major US carriers. Carrier support may vary.

Privacy

We do not sell, rent, or share your phone number or message content with third parties for marketing purposes. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
